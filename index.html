Pick a dataset, pick a field, and ask a question of it. The explorer goes through the pile one card at a time, the same way you would by hand, and shows each step.

An unofficial study aid. It is not made by or affiliated with IIT Madras. The sample cards and most questions are original practice material, and the cards are smaller than the course's datasets. Questions tagged AQ are reworded from the course's ungraded activity questions, for personal practice only. Check the course videos for the exact field names used in your assessments.
